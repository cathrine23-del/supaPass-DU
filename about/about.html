ABOUT SUPAPASS DU

Your campus. Your events. Your pass.

SupaPass DU is a campus event discovery and ticket booking platform designed to make it easier for Daystar University students to discover, book and keep track of campus events.

Instead of searching through different social media posts, group chats or announcements, students can use one central platform to find upcoming events, explore categories and reserve their places.

HOW IT WORKS

Campus events, made simple.

From discovery to your digital pass.

01

Discover

Find events happening around Daystar using categories, filters or search.

02

Book

Choose an event and reserve your place through SupaPass DU.

03

Attend

Keep your digital ticket and use it when you arrive at the event.
